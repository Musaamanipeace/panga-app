Panga – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Panga is a personal productivity app (tasks, notes, links, reminders and more). This page explains what happens to your data.

Where your data lives

Your content is stored in your own browser on your own device. The Panga developer does not run a server that receives or stores your tasks, notes or other content.

Sign in with Google and Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google, Panga uses Google Sign-In to read your basic profile (your name and email address, shown in the app) and asks for the drive.file permission. That permission lets Panga see and manage only the files and folders that Panga itself creates in your Google Drive: a folder named “panga” containing a backup file named “panga-backup.json”. Panga cannot see any of your other Drive files.

  • The backup file is saved in your own Google Drive and is only readable by you and anyone you share it with.
  • Google access tokens are kept in your browser’s memory only and are discarded when you sign out, reload, or the token expires.
  • Your name and email are used only to display who is signed in. They are not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • You can delete the backup from within Panga, or delete the “panga” folder in Google Drive, at any time. You can also revoke Panga’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Panga’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional AI and voice features

If you turn on an AI assistant or transcription feature and provide your own API key or server, the text or audio you send to that feature is transmitted to the provider you chose (for example an AI or speech service), under that provider’s own terms and privacy policy. These features are off unless you set them up.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, and we do not use data from Google APIs to train AI models.

Deleting your data

You can clear Panga’s data by clearing the site’s storage in your browser, and remove the Drive backup as described above.
