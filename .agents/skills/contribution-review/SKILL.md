1. Check the PR: body has a closing keyword referencing an issue (`close(s)/fix(es)/resolve(s) #N`); follows CONTRIBUTING.md; small focused commits; no unrequested scope or extra files.
2. Summarize findings in the standard completion-report format.
3. Never merge external PRs without my explicit approval.
